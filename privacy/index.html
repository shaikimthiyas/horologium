Data Sovereignty & Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 // Lead Architect: Shaik Imthiyas

1. Zero-Knowledge Client Chronometry

Horologium operates on a zero-knowledge client architecture. All timezone calculations, subsolar meridian projections, and business overlap computations execute locally within your browser engine using the standard ECMAScript Intl.DateTimeFormat API.

Your geographical location, coordinates, and local timezone selections are never transmitted to third parties, advertising networks, or telemetry trackers.

2. Cookie-Free Architecture

Horologium does not set or read any HTTP tracking cookies. No third-party analytics pixels, fingerprinting scripts, or cross-site tracking mechanisms are embedded into any page of this platform.

3. Local Persistence & Client Storage

When you pin custom telemetry stations, toggle between Obsidian Rhodium and Silvered Dial themes, or queue offline transmissions, data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage or IndexedDB. You retain complete ownership of this data and may purge it at any time via your browser settings.

4. International Chronometry Standards & RFC 9557 Compliance

All timestamps, zone offsets, and temporal representations adhere strictly to RFC 9557 (Date and Time on the Internet: Timestamps with Additional Information) and RFC 5905 (Network Time Protocol Version 4). Zone identifiers follow the official IANA Time Zone Database (TZDB 2024b).

5. Open Source Governance & Contact

Horologium is open-source software distributed under the terms of the MIT License. The complete source code and automated test suites are publicly auditable on GitHub.
