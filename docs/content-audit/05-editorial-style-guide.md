# Horologium Editorial Style Guide

> **Core Philosophy:** Every word must justify its existence. Speak with the precision of a Swiss master watchmaker and the technical clarity of an enterprise systems engineer.

---

## 1. Voice & Tone Principles

| Dimension | Standard | Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Voice** | Precise, composed, dignified, intelligent, human | Hype-driven, robotic, sci-fi military, over-enthusiastic |
| **Tone** | Understated confidence, clarity, reverence for astronomy and horology | "Cutting-edge", "game-changing", "revolutionary" |
| **Verbs** | Direct, active verbs (`Open`, `Compare`, `Calculate`, `Export`) | Vague verbs (`Experience`, `Unlock`, `Unleash`, `Supercharge`) |
| **Punctuation** | Standard English punctuation; conservative use of dashes | Cascading em-dashes (`—`), double exclamation points (`!!`) |
| **Capitalization** | Sentence case for buttons, headings, and labels | Unnecessary ALL CAPS for entire sentences or decorative headings |

## 3. Microcopy Rules

- **Buttons:** Start with a verb describing the direct action (`Copy Time`, `Save Station`, `Run Probe`, `Download JSON`).
- **Empty States:** State what is missing, why, and what action to take next.
  - *Example:* "No saved timezones. Select a timezone from the directory to pin it to your dashboard."
- **Status Indicators:** Always reflect real, measured system state.
  - *Example:* `Connected · Round-trip delay: 18ms` instead of `INSTANTANEOUS QUANTUM SYNC`.
- **Error Messages:** Plain language explaining what happened and how to resolve it.
  - *Example:* "Unable to connect to synchronization server. Reverting to local oscillator drift mode."
