Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 · Created by Shaik Imthiyas

1. Zero-Knowledge Client Processing

Horologium operates on a zero-knowledge client architecture. All timezone calculations, daylight boundaries, and meeting overlap computations execute locally within your browser using standard JavaScript APIs.

Your location, coordinates, and timezone selections are never transmitted to external servers, advertising networks, or analytics trackers.

2. No Tracking Cookies or Analytics

Horologium does not set or read tracking cookies. No third-party analytics pixels, fingerprinting scripts, or cross-site tracking tools are used anywhere on this website.

3. Local Browser Storage

When you save favorite cities or toggle dark/light themes, that information is stored only on your device using localStorage. You retain complete ownership and can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

4. International Standards Compliance

All timestamps, zone offsets, and temporal representations adhere strictly to RFC 9557 (Date and Time on the Internet) and RFC 5905 (Network Time Protocol). Timezone definitions follow the official IANA Time Zone Database.

5. Open Source & Contact

Horologium is open-source software distributed under the terms of the MIT License. The complete source code is publicly auditable on GitHub.
